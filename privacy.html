Privacy Policy

Photo & Image Compressor

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Photo & Image Compressor ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the App. The App is developed and published by Ali Tailor. By using the App you agree to this policy.

Short version: Your photos are compressed on your device and are never uploaded to us. We do not ask you to create an account. The App shows ads, and the advertising partners described below may collect device and advertising data to serve those ads.

1. Information We Collect

  • Your photos and images: All image processing (compression, resizing, format conversion) happens locally on your device. We do not upload, transmit, view, or store your photos on any server. Your original photos remain on your device and are not modified by the App.
  • No account data: The App does not require registration. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.
  • App settings and history: Preferences and a local compression history are stored only on your device and are removed when you clear the App data or uninstall the App.

2. Advertising and Third-Party Data

The App is free and supported by advertising delivered through Google AdMob and, where enabled, mediated advertising sources including Unity Ads. These advertising partners may automatically collect certain information in order to provide and measure ads, such as:

  • Device identifiers, including the advertising ID;
  • IP address and approximate (non-precise) location derived from it;
  • Device type, operating system version, and language;
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, and similar events).

This data is collected and processed by the advertising partners under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your personal data from them; we may receive aggregated, non-identifying reports about ad performance.

  • Google / AdMob Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses data in partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Unity Ads Privacy Policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

3. Internet Permission

The App requests internet access only to load and display advertisements. The image compression features of the App work fully offline.

4. Device Permissions

  • Photos / media: accessed through your device's system photo picker, only for the images you choose to compress.
  • Camera (optional): used only when you choose to take a new photo to compress. It is not used in the background.
  • Internet / network state: used only for advertising.

5. Your Choices Regarding Personalized Ads

You can limit or reset ad personalization at any time through your device settings, for example Settings → Privacy → Ads (Android), where you can opt out of personalized advertising or reset your advertising ID. Where required by law, the App presents a consent request before personalized ads are shown.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

7. Your Rights (EEA, UK, and US State Laws)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or in certain US states (such as California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of personal data, or to opt out of the "sale" or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising. Because the App itself does not collect personal data, requests concerning advertising data should be directed to the advertising partners listed above. You may also contact us and we will assist where we can.

8. Data Security and Retention

We do not operate servers that store your content. Data created by the App (settings and history) stays on your device and is retained until you delete it or uninstall the App. Data collected by advertising partners is retained according to their own policies.
